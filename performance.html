UNDERSTAND YOUR ESTIMATE

How the calculation works

Semester GPA = Σ(credit hours × grade points) ÷ graded credit hours

Estimated CGPA = (previous CGPA × previous GPA credits + semester weighted points) ÷ (previous GPA credits + graded semester credits)

Results are rounded only for display. Incomplete grades produce a provisional estimate. This planner does not apply repeat-course replacement, transfer, exemption or special academic-status rules. Use your official result slip for confirmed results.
